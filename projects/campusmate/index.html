01 / THE CONTEXT

The problem worth solving.

New students need context about campus spaces, not just a map pin: where to go, what is inside, and what a room actually looks like.

The approach

Combine campus navigation with detailed location content, room imagery, rich media and a data-backed mobile experience.

03 / ENGINEERING JUDGMENT

The decisions behind the build.

01

Connect map and context

A location opens meaningful details, amenities and media instead of ending at a marker.

02

Design around mobile

Keep navigation, media and location actions accessible within a compact touch interface.

03

Keep exploration available

A documented local-data fallback lets the interface remain usable during network interruptions.
