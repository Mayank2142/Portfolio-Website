01 / THE CONTEXT

The problem worth solving.

A seat grid is straightforward until customers compete for one seat, abandon checkout, or accept a waitlist offer at the same moment.

The approach

Model inventory per show, enforce ownership and expiry at the database boundary, and connect the interface to live invalidation and recoverable background work.

02 / UNDER THE HOOD

05Durable expiry and notification jobs

03 / ENGINEERING JUDGMENT

The decisions behind the build.

01

Make the database authoritative

Atomic state conditions validate the seat, hold owner and expiry before confirmation. UI state is not the booking lock.

02

Recover abandoned work

Expiry is enforced during reads and by a background worker, with durable jobs for interrupted processing.

03

Keep the queue fair

A cancellation can become a single-use offer to the next eligible queue member instead of a free-for-all.

05 / THE TAKEAWAY

What I’m taking forward.
